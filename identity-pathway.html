Prevention & Early Intervention

Identity Pathway
— Your IP to Success

Disrupting the school-to-prison pipeline for young males aged 12–25 at risk of exclusion or already caught in the youth justice system.

The Problem This Programme Solves

Most interventions address behaviour. TIM addresses identity.

Most school-based interventions address behaviour. They set targets, manage consequences, and manage exits. What they rarely do is ask the young person who they actually are — and help them build an answer that doesn't depend on the peer group, the gang, or the label the system has given them.

That absence of identity is what makes a young person recruitable. It is what makes the street, the peer group, and ultimately the justice system fill the vacuum. The Identity Pathway addresses that vacuum directly.

How It Works

The Identity Pathway does not run workshops about prison. It takes young people into one.

Three elements combine to create an encounter that changes the trajectory.

01

The Environment

Young people from schools and PRUs are brought into HMP & YOI Brinsford — a working prison. Not a simulation, not a managed tour. They experience the reality of correctional life firsthand. The setting does half the work before anyone speaks.

02

The Messenger

Sessions are co-delivered by young adults currently serving time at Brinsford who have undergone TIM's own programme. They are trained as co-facilitators. They share the truth about what brought them there, what it cost, and what they would do differently. That credibility cannot be replicated by a professional facilitator.

03

The Method

Three workshop pillars — Identity, Purpose, and Wisdom — form the content of every session. Young people are helped to examine who they are, discover a sense of direction, and build the practical wisdom to navigate the choices in front of them. All grounded in the I.N.V.E.S.T. framework.
