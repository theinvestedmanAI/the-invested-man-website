Get Involved

Work with TIM.

TIM works across the full school-to-prison pipeline — from early intervention in schools to rehabilitation inside prisons. Whatever your role, there is a way to be part of redirecting lives and breaking cycles.

Schools, PRUs & Youth Offending Services

Bring the Identity Pathway to your students.

The Identity Pathway is designed for young men and boys aged 12–25 at risk of exclusion or already engaged with the youth justice system. Sessions are delivered in schools, PRUs, alternative provisions, youth institutions, and secure units — co-facilitated by young adults with lived experience of the criminal justice system. If you work with young people who are heading in the wrong direction, this is the intervention.

Prison Governors & Operational Leads

Partner with TIM on resettlement.

TIM co-designs with your Senior Leadership Team and integrates with your existing resettlement pathways. We don't come in to run a session and leave. We build something that continues to work after we're gone — because the men we train become the programme.
